# Vibe Dining (Chow)

**Lagos-first occasion-based restaurant discovery app.**

Vibe Dining is a restaurant discovery platform designed for urban Lagos diners aged 22-45 who want trusted recommendations for specific occasions — not a filtered list of options.

## What makes it different

- **Occasion-based discovery** — find restaurants for "first date," "birthday dinner," "instagrammable spot" — not just generic categories
- **Comparative rating system** — pairwise judgments instead of unreliable star ratings
- **Curated editorial voice** — every restaurant earns its place for a specific dining theme
- **Warm Editorial Maximalism** — design that feels like a trusted Lagos city guide that got a bold rebrand

## Design System

- **Typography:** Canela (display/editorial) + PolySans (UI/body) — self-hosted in `public/fonts/`
- **Palette:** Food-inspired — maroon, orange, sherbet, peach
- **Direction:** Not corporate, not chaotic — "someone with taste made this"
